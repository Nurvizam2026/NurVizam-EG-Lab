DRAWING METHOD

Think in stages

  1. 01
    Start from a simple position

    Establish the solid's geometry and its principal views before introducing inclination.

  2. 02
    Introduce the angle

    Rotate the solid about a defined axis or hinge, following the condition stated in the problem.

  3. 03
    Project corresponding points

    Transfer coordinates consistently to the front, top and side views.

  4. 04
    Check before finalizing

    Verify dimensions, alignment, visible edges and the projection convention used in your course.

Learning model note: this first version shows a wireframe orthographic projection of a prism rotated to an angle and lifted so it does not pass below HP.
