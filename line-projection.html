ENGINEERING GRAPHICS PRINCIPLE

How the rotating-line method works

  1. Assume a simple position. First keep the line parallel to VP and incline it to HP by α. Its front view shows true length; its top view is shorter and parallel to XY.
  2. Rotate the top view. Rotate the top-view projection about A to the calculated plan angle required by β. Do not simply set the top-view angle equal to β: β is the line's angle with VP, not generally the angle of its top view to XY.
  3. Project the final front view. From the rotated top view, draw projectors and preserve the endpoint height difference from the assumed front view. The resulting FV and TV represent the same final 3D line.

Feasibility check: for a line inclined to both planes, the specified angles must satisfy sin²α + sin²β ≤ 1. If this condition is violated, no line direction can satisfy both angles simultaneously. The module reports this rather than drawing a misleading solution.
